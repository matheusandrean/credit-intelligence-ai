# Model Card — Credit Intelligence AI Risk Model

Following the spirit of [Model Cards for Model Reporting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993)
(Mitchell et al., 2019).

## Model Details

- **Developed by**: Matheus Marcondes, as a portfolio project.
- **Model type**: Binary classification (90-day default prediction),
  two candidates trained and compared:
  1. **Logistic Regression** (`sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression`,
     `class_weight="balanced"`, `C=0.5`) — the interpretable baseline.
  2. **LightGBM** (`lightgbm.LGBMClassifier`, 400 estimators, early
     stopping) — the non-linear challenger.
- **Champion selection**: by out-of-time (OOT) test ROC-AUC, recorded in
  `models/model_metadata.json`. In the last full training run, **Logistic
  Regression** was selected as champion (OOT AUC 0.836 vs LightGBM's
  0.834 — a near-tie, itself a meaningful finding: see "Champion vs
  Challenger" below).
- **Calibration**: Platt scaling (`sigmoid`) or isotonic regression, fit on
  the **validation split only**; the platform defaults to sigmoid unless
  isotonic improves validation Brier score by >5% relative, because
  isotonic regression's step-function nature can silently absorb small
  input changes in the what-if/stress-testing tools (a documented,
  deliberate trade-off — see `src/models/calibration.py`).
- **Version**: tied to `models/model_metadata.json` (regenerated by
  `make train` + the calibration step).

## Intended Use

**Primary intended use**: decision-**support** for credit analysts and risk
managers investigating a synthetic demonstration portfolio — never
autonomous credit decisioning. See [`RESPONSIBLE_AI.md`](RESPONSIBLE_AI.md).

**Out of scope**: any real credit decision, any deployment against real
customer data without full independent revalidation, and any use of the
score/band as a commercial pricing policy (both are explicitly
demonstrative).

## Training Data

100,000 synthetic customers, temporally split (not randomly) to avoid
look-ahead bias — see [`docs/TEMPORAL_VALIDATION.md`](docs/TEMPORAL_VALIDATION.md)
and [`DATA_CARD.md`](DATA_CARD.md):

| Split | Window | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Train | 2023-01 to 2024-06 | 63,234 |
| Validation | 2024-07 to 2024-12 | 20,180 |
| Test (OOT) | 2025-01 to 2025-06 | 16,586 |

## Features

38 columns in the modeling dataset: 24 raw + 6 engineered features + 2
categorical (age band, payment history) + identifiers/target/split — see
[`FEATURE_DICTIONARY.md`](FEATURE_DICTIONARY.md) for the full list and
formulas. No protected/sensitive characteristic is used, directly or as a
proxy (enforced by `src/data/validation.py`).

## Metrics (real, measured — see `reports/model_comparison.json`)

Evaluated with a full metric suite, never accuracy alone (accuracy is
uninformative on this ≈8% base-rate imbalanced problem):

| Metric (OOT test) | Logistic Regression (champion) | LightGBM (challenger) |
|---|---:|---:|
| ROC-AUC | **0.8357** | 0.8339 |
| Gini | 0.6715 | 0.6679 |
| KS statistic | 0.5259 | 0.5243 |
| PR-AUC | 0.3308 | 0.3133 |
| Brier score (raw, uncalibrated) | 0.1816 | 0.1812 |
| Lift @ top decile | 3.98x | 3.97x |
| Recall @ top decile | 39.8% | 39.7% |
| Recall @ top 2 deciles | 63.4% | 63.5% |

**Calibration** (champion model, OOT test): Brier score improved from
**0.1816 (raw)** to **0.0655 (Platt-scaled)**.

**Champion vs Challenger note**: the two models are within ~0.002 AUC of
each other on OOT data. This is a realistic and honest outcome for this
feature set — it does not indicate a bug, and it is a legitimate reason to
prefer the simpler, fully interpretable Logistic Regression as champion in
a governance-sensitive setting, all else being close to equal.

## Ethical Considerations

- Trained exclusively on synthetic data; see [`DATA_CARD.md`](DATA_CARD.md)
  for how default risk was constructed and why the AUC was deliberately
  tuned down from an initial ~0.95 (implausibly good) to a realistic
  ~0.83-0.85.
- No protected/sensitive attribute is present or usable, directly or via
  proxy — see [`RESPONSIBLE_AI.md`](RESPONSIBLE_AI.md).
- Age band is used as a coarse behavioral segment; in a real deployment,
  age-based scoring may be subject to specific fair-lending regulatory
  review that this demonstration does not perform.

## Caveats and Recommendations

- **Reject inference**: the model is trained only on originated accounts —
  there is no "declined applicant" population, so the model may not
  generalize to a true through-the-door population. This is a known,
  unaddressed limitation of the demonstration (see
  [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)).
- **No independent validation**: this model has not undergone independent
  model validation, legal review, or regulatory approval of any kind.
- **Illustrative score/band**: the 300-900 score and A-E bands are a
  standard points-to-double-odds transformation of PD, calibrated to
  reference points chosen for this demonstration — not a real commercial
  scorecard.
- **SHAP explains the raw model**: SHAP values are computed on the
  pre-calibration decision function; calibration is monotonic, so
  direction/ranking of drivers remains valid, but the exact SHAP magnitude
  does not map 1:1 to the calibrated PD's marginal sensitivity.
- Retrain and re-run the full evaluation suite (`make train`) whenever the
  feature pipeline changes, or when `detect_drift` reports a PSI above the
  "Monitor" threshold — see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
